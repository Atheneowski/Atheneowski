# 🎯 Engineering OKR Tracking Framework

## 🚀 Introduction
Objectives and Key Results (**OKRs**) help engineering teams align goals with business priorities, measure impact, and drive continuous improvement. This framework provides a structured approach for defining, tracking, and reviewing engineering OKRs effectively.

---

## 🛠 How to Use This Framework
- **Define**: Establish clear, measurable OKRs.
- **Track**: Use GitHub Discussions, Issues, or Project Boards to monitor progress.
- **Review**: Regularly assess performance, iterate, and adapt based on outcomes.

---

## 📌 OKR Structure
A standard OKR consists of:
- **Objective**: A high-level, qualitative goal.
- **Key Results**: Measurable outcomes that indicate progress toward the objective.

**Example:**
**Objective:** Improve engineering deployment efficiency.
- **KR1:** Reduce deployment time from 1 hour to 30 minutes.
- **KR2:** Decrease rollback incidents by 20%.
- **KR3:** Automate 80% of manual deployment processes.

---

## 📊 Tracking OKRs
### ✅ GitHub-Based Tracking Methods
- **GitHub Issues:** Track individual key results with labels and milestones.
- **Project Boards:** Organize OKRs by status (To Do, In Progress, Achieved).
- **GitHub Discussions:** Facilitate async team check-ins and feedback loops. Also check out this [general weekly reporting template](https://github.com/Atheneowski/Atheneowski/blob/main/Engineering%20OKR%20Tracking%20Framework/General%20GitHub%20Discussions%20Template.md).

### 🏆 Progress Review Cadence
- **Weekly Check-ins:** Short updates on progress blockers and wins.
- **Quarterly Reviews:** Assess impact, refine objectives, and plan next cycle.
- **Annual Reflection:** Identify long-term trends and improvements.

---

## 📂 Engineering OKR Examples
### **🚀 Productivity & Efficiency**
- **O:** Reduce engineering toil by streamlining workflows.
  - **KR1:** Implement CI/CD improvements to cut build times by 40%.
  - **KR2:** Reduce context-switching by consolidating tool integrations.
  - **KR3:** Increase automated test coverage to 85%.

### **🛡️ Reliability & Performance**
- **O:** Improve system uptime and stability.
  - **KR1:** Maintain **99.99% uptime** across all services.
  - **KR2:** Reduce major incidents from 5 per quarter to 2.
  - **KR3:** Improve MTTR (Mean Time to Recovery) from 60 minutes to 20 minutes.

### **🔍 Developer Experience**
- **O:** Enhance internal documentation and onboarding experience.
  - **KR1:** Improve documentation engagement (e.g., 50% more page views).
  - **KR2:** Reduce new hire onboarding time from 3 weeks to 1.5 weeks.
  - **KR3:** Gather and act on quarterly developer feedback.
